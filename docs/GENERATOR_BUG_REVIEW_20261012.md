# Generator instruction contamination — 12 October 2026

A connected review of a website-design generator reproduced an incorrect industry choice and unconditional trust, business-detail, performance and consent instructions. Adding a later caution did not retract earlier template requirements.

The candidate correction uses complete industry terms, qualifies evidence suggestions where they originate, omits unapproved public business details, separates optional marketing consent and tests the complete handoff. The web candidate passed 51 targeted tests; the native candidate passed 62 tests and manifest validation. Existing saved drafts are not rewritten.

This supports BUG-0024 as a candidate repair, not a completed production migration. Isolated review-service acceptance, hosted save/reconnect, publisher acceptance and production cutover are separate evidence boundaries. No customer loss or business outcome is inferred.
