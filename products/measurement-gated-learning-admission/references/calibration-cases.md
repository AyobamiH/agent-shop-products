# Calibration cases

- Verified predecessor has comparable mature measurements: apply the remaining resource gates before admission.
- Exact effect equivalence exists but no comparable observation: defer the challenger.
- Metric value is zero with status unavailable: treat performance as unknown.
- Verified publication belongs to another provider account: reject equivalence.
- A sidecar would fix lineage by changing original receipt bytes: preserve the receipt and reject that rewrite.
- Backlog ceiling is ten and only three candidates qualify: admit at most the qualifying bounded set; do not invent seven fillers.
